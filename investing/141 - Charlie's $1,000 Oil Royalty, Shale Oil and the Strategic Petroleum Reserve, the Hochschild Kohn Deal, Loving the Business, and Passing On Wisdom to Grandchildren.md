# Charlie's $1,000 Oil Royalty, Shale Oil and the Strategic Petroleum Reserve, the Hochschild Kohn Deal, Loving the Business, and Passing On Wisdom to Grandchildren

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SZMvFi9vsLY

Recorded: most likely 2022 Berkshire annual meeting (moderate confidence)

> Note: The original YouTube title is "Charlie Munger: How I Turned $1,000 Into $70,000 Every Single Year". It is misleading: there is no investing method here. In a short aside at the start, Munger mentions an old oil royalty (Bakersfield "or wherever it is") that he apparently paid about $1,000 for and that still pays him $70,000 a year; Buffett then compares it with his father's royalties and moves on to shale oil, the Strategic Petroleum Reserve, Occidental, a question about funny stories and the hardest part of the job, the Hochschild Kohn purchase, and a question about passing on wisdom to grandchildren. The clip is most likely from the **2022** Berkshire annual meeting (moderate confidence). The clues: Munger is alive and on stage; Berkshire "like[s] Occidental's position in the Permian" (Berkshire disclosed its Occidental stake in early 2022); the U.S. is "taking down" the Strategic Petroleum Reserve (the large drawdown began in spring 2022); U.S. production is put at "11 and a fraction million barrels"; the negative oil price (April 2020) is referred to as past. The 2023 meeting is the main alternative. Buffett says it is 3:30, so this is from the end of the afternoon session.
