> Note: The original YouTube title is "Charlie Munger: McDonald's Is A Better Educator Than Universities". It is only partly accurate. Munger does say, in a short middle segment, that he once told a major university that McDonald's "succeeded better as an educator than the people in the university did", meaning it teaches marginal young workers to show up on time and take responsibility.
