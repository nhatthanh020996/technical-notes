# The Luxury of Choosing His Work, Recommending Greg as CEO at Year-End, and Keeping Every Berkshire Share

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1X6Nbf6zbII

Recorded: 2025 Berkshire annual meeting (high confidence)

> Note: The original YouTube title is "Warren Buffett: Why You Should Always Invest Like An Owner". It is misleading: Buffett gives no advice about investing like an owner. The clip is the end of the meeting in which he announces that he will recommend to the board that Greg become CEO at year-end, says Greg did not know beforehand, and says he has no intention of selling a single Berkshire share. Before that, he reflects on having been able to choose his work and the people he works with. This is the **2025** Berkshire annual meeting (May 3, 2025; high confidence). The clues: the surprise announcement that Greg would become CEO at year-end (Greg Abel; the surname is not said in the clip), the board meeting "tomorrow", the 11 directors including his children Howard and Susie, and "Becky" (Becky Quick; surname not said in the clip) about to ask questions.
