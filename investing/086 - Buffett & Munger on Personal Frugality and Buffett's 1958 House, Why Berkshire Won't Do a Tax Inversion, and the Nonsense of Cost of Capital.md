## Part 3: Cost of capital: "we're right and they're wrong" ([05:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NW3UPVF5iE0&t=346))

**Warren Buffett:** Yeah, yeah, it's really true. I mean, that's another thing. I've been on boards and the CFO comes in and explains why we're doing this, and it always gets down to, you know, "It exceeds our cost of capital." And he doesn't know what the hell [unclear: "it's caused to capitalism"], I don't know. The real test, over time, is whether the capital we retain produces more than a dollar [of] market values as we go along.
