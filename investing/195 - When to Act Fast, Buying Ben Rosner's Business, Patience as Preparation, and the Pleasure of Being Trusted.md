# When to Act Fast, Buying Ben Rosner's Business, Patience as Preparation, and the Pleasure of Being Trusted

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W5R_F2JIYKQ

Recorded: Berkshire annual meeting, 2024 or 2025, probably 2025 (low-moderate confidence)

> Note: The original YouTube title is "Warren Buffett: Buy Fast When Stocks Are Cheap". It is misleading. Buffett does not talk about stocks being cheap or about buying stocks at all. A shareholder asks whether acting fast instead of being patient has ever paid off, and Buffett answers with the story of buying a private business: Ben Rosner's company, offered for $6 million with $2 million in cash, $2 million of Philadelphia real estate and $2 million a year of pre-tax earnings. His point is that you are patient in waiting for the call, and not patient once it comes. Greg Abel adds that patience is used for preparation, and Buffett ends on the pleasure of being trusted. The upload date (2026) is not the event date. The setting is a Berkshire annual meeting after Charlie Munger's death in November 2023: Munger is spoken of in the past tense ("both Charlie and I, we just enjoyed"), Greg Abel answers alongside Buffett and mentions Ajit Jain, and Buffett hands over to "Becky" (Becky Quick). The year is **2024 or 2025**; 2025 is a guess (low-moderate confidence). Buffett says the Rosner call came "in 1966", and that Rosner's late partner's widow was "Mrs. Annenberg"; the name of the New York lawyer appears in the captions as both "Paul Steiner" and "Will Felstiner" and is kept as captioned.
