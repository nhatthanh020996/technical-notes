# The 25-5 Priority List Myth, Living Simply, and Decision Fatigue with Caffeine and Sugar

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KylZCuzPgjc

Recorded: a Berkshire annual meeting, probably in the second half of the 2010s (low confidence)

> Note: The original YouTube title is "Warren Buffett: Why Productivity Advice Is Bullsh*t". It is misleading: Buffett never calls productivity advice anything of the sort. A questioner asks about the widely circulated story that Buffett writes down 25 goals, picks the top five and avoids the other 20. Buffett says, politely, that it "really isn't the case": it sounds like a good method, but he has never made such a list ("maybe I'll start"), and he and Charlie simply live very simple lives. Munger then adds, half-jokingly, that modern psychology (decision fatigue; caffeine and sugar helping decisions) shows that Buffett's habitual, autopilot life is "absolutely ideal for human cognition".
