> Note: The original YouTube title is "Warren Buffett: How Americans Can Rapidly Build Wealth". It is misleading: there is no advice on building wealth, rapidly or otherwise. This is Buffett's slide presentation on American history: the country's growth since 1789 (a rough $1 billion of wealth then versus more than $100 trillion of household wealth now, about 5,000 for one in real terms), the Louisiana Purchase as a "comp", the Civil War, the 1929 crash and the Great Depression (with his own family's story and his praise of the FDIC), the 1954–55 worry that the market was "1929 all over again" and Ben Graham's Senate testimony, and progress toward the equality promised in 1776. The only investing point is that someone who just bought "a cross-section of America" in 1955 got about $100 for every dollar, over roughly 65 years. The uploader's description says Buffett and Munger "discuss" investing, but only Buffett speaks; Charlie Munger is mentioned but does not speak. The clip is from the **2020** Berkshire annual meeting (high confidence), the virtual meeting held during COVID-19 with Greg Abel next to Buffett.
