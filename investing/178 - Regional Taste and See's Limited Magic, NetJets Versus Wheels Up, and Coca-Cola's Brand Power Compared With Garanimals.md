# Regional Taste and See's Limited Magic, NetJets Versus Wheels Up, and Coca-Cola's Brand Power Compared With Garanimals

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsNkDySWJCQ

Recorded: most likely 2023 Berkshire annual meeting (high confidence)

> Note: The original YouTube title is "Warren Buffett: How To Profit From Brand Stocks". It is misleading: there is no advice about buying "brand stocks". Buffett talks about what Berkshire has learned from owning brands: regional quirks in taste (Dr Pepper in Dallas, milk vs. dark chocolate), See's Candy's "limited magic" outside the West and the failed experiments to expand it, NetJets as the Ferrari of private aviation compared with the collapsing Wheels Up, why heirs are the ones paying for a NetJets share (his Aunt Alice story), how Charlie was talked into NetJets, Coca-Cola's recognisable bottle and global reach, and Garanimals and Fruit of the Loom as products that sell through Walmart without commanding a premium. The clip is most likely from the **2023** Berkshire annual meeting (high confidence). The clues: Buffett says See's "is 101 years now" (founded 1921); Wheels Up stock "came out at $10 a couple years ago" and was "selling at 48 cents the other day", which fits spring 2023; he mentions taking a NetJets flight to Tokyo (his April 2023 Japan trip); and Charlie is alive and on stage. The upload date (March 2025) is not the event date.
