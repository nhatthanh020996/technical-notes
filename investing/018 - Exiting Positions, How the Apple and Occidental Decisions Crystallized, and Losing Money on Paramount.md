**Warren Buffett:** Thank you. ## Part 6: Paramount was Buffett's decision alone, and it lost money ([10:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g2jSLsQQC4M&t=651))

**Warren Buffett:** Oh, incidentally, I should just throw this out since there's been speculation on it. We've sold [...] I was 100% responsible for the Paramount decision. I read speculation that either Ted or Todd had some involvement in that. No, it was 100% my decision. And we sold it all, and we lost quite a bit of money, and that happens in this business too.

But actually owning Paramount made me think even further. I like to think deeper, but I certainly thought harder even about the whole question of what people do with their leisure time, and what the governing principles are of running an entertainment business of any sort, whether it's sports or movies or whatever it might be. And I think I'm smarter now than I was [a] year or two, a couple years ago, but I also think I'm poor[er] because I acquired the knowledge in the manner I did. But I just want to be very clear that, A, we lost money on Paramount, and B, I did it all by myself, folks.
