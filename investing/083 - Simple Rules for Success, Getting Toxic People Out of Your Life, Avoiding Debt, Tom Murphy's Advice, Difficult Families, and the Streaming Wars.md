# Simple Rules for Success, Getting Toxic People Out of Your Life, Avoiding Debt, Tom Murphy's Advice, Difficult Families, and the Streaming Wars

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qcxbQxfSDzk

Recorded: most likely 2023 Berkshire annual meeting (moderate-high confidence)

> Note: The original YouTube title is "Charlie Munger: Simple Mistake That Wrecks Your Portfolio". It is misleading: there is no single portfolio-wrecking mistake in the clip, and most of the talking is done by Buffett. The closest thing is Buffett's advice to avoid mistakes "that take you out of the game", spend less than you earn and stay away from credit-card debt. The rest is life advice: Munger's simple rules (spend less than you earn, invest shrewdly, avoid toxic people, keep learning, defer gratification) and getting toxic people out of your life fast; Buffett's "write your obituary and live up to it"; Tom Murphy's advice ("tell someone to go to hell tomorrow", "praise by name, criticize by category"); what to do when the difficult people are your family (the MacArthur siblings); and a question on the streaming wars, where they decline to give stock advice and describe the movie, streaming and Broadway businesses as tough. The clip is most likely from the **2023** Berkshire annual meeting (moderate-high confidence). The clues: Munger is present (he died in November 2023); the "streaming wars" question and the remark that "it's not good news when any company passes a dividend or cuts a dividend dramatically" (Paramount, a Berkshire holding, cut its dividend in 2023); theaters "are now doing 70% of the business [they] did before the pandemic"; and Buffett speaks of Tom Murphy (who died in 2022) in the past tense, "I watched him for 50 years", with "maybe Jr. who's here". The earlier segments are not independently dated, but nothing in them conflicts with 2023.
