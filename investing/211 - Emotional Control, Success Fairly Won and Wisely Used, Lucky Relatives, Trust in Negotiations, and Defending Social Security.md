# Emotional Control, Success Fairly Won and Wisely Used, Lucky Relatives, Trust in Negotiations, and Defending Social Security

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pzuxDLR3_Ww

Recorded: Part 5 2005 Berkshire annual meeting (high confidence); Parts 1–4 Berkshire annual meeting(s), year uncertain, possibly the same meeting (low confidence)

> Note: The original YouTube title is "Charlie Munger: Stock Market Investors Often Behave Stupidly". It is misleading: Munger never says this, and nobody talks about stock market investors. It is Buffett (not Munger) who says "we see very smart people do very stupid things", and his example is rich people who lever up and lose everything. Munger's own contributions are about temperament, behaving well ("success so fairly won and wisely used"), working for Buffett's grandfather, and a strong defence of Social Security. The longest section is Buffett arguing against cutting Social Security benefits.
>
> This is a **compilation** of several clips. Part 5 is dated by Buffett himself ("the world in 2005 is much different than the world in 1937"), and by the $90,000 Social Security wage cap, 15% tax on dividends and capital gains, the fighting in Iraq, and a Republican administration taking on Social Security; it is the **2005** Berkshire annual meeting (high confidence). Part 3 is from an annual meeting (Buffett mentions a family gathering "yesterday" at "annual meeting time"), but the year is not identifiable; Parts 1, 2 and 4 have no date clues and could be from the same or other meetings (low confidence). Parts 1, 4 and 5 start mid-answer. Upload date: 2024-10-21 (not the event date).

**Warren Buffett:** Negotiations that drag out have a tendency that they're more likely to blow up, for some reason. **Warren Buffett:** And not everybody's wired the same way. **Charlie Munger:** Well, that's the view from [the] Democratic chairman. And the odd part of Berkshire on this issue is that the right-wing Republican who is speaking feels more strongly than Warren that the Republicans are out of their cotton-picking minds to be taking on this issue right now.
