# Buffett & Munger on Advice to a 30-Year-Old, Rational Insurance Underwriting, Envy, Partnership Portfolios, and Graham's Book

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pHAPr5IC3No

> Note: The original YouTube title is "Warren Buffett: Advice To My Younger Self". It is misleading. Only the first question (about two and a half minutes) asks what they would tell themselves 50 years ago, and Charlie Munger gives most of that answer ("keep plugging along", "stay rational", "work where you're turned on"); Buffett adds that they were lucky to find what they liked early. The rest of the clip is unrelated to advice to a younger self: why Berkshire's insurance pricing stays rational (shrinking National Indemnity's traditional business by about 80 percent, leaving catastrophe insurance when prices were wrong), social proof and envy, a request to publish the old partnership portfolios, and the books that shaped Buffett (*The Intelligent Investor*, Phil Fisher). The clip is cut together from several Q&A exchanges, apparently from one Berkshire annual meeting; the most likely year is **2013** (low–moderate confidence). The clues: Buffett says they "just" read the Joe Kennedy biography (David Nasaw's *The Patriarch* came out in late 2012); Becky Quick is reading emailed questions (the format used from 2006); Munger is alive; catastrophe prices are described as currently too low, which fits the soft cat market of roughly 2013–2017. A questioner asking how they changed "over the last 50 years" could also point to 2015 (50 years since Buffett took control of Berkshire), so 2014–2015 cannot be ruled out. The books segment (Part 5) follows an obvious cut and may come from a different meeting.
