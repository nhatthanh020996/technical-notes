# Reading Balance Sheets, Learning From Charlie and Other Teachers, and Government's Role in Energy Policy

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0_UwNBxSxzY

Recorded: after Charlie Munger's death (November 2023), most likely the 2024 or 2025 Berkshire annual meeting (low confidence); the clip is a heavily spliced compilation

> Note: The original YouTube title is "Warren Buffett: Why You Must Read Balance Sheets". It is misleading: only the first minute is about balance sheets (Buffett says he looks at ten years of balance sheets before the income account, because they're harder to play games with). The rest is about Charlie Munger and other teachers, and mostly about why government and private enterprise have to combine on energy policy, using World War II and the Interstate Highway System as examples. The clip is a compilation of short fragments, often cut mid-sentence, so many passages don't connect. Buffett speaks of Charlie in the past tense ("Charlie was probably the best person you could imagine"), so this is after Munger's death in November 2023. The energy-policy passages fit the utility and wildfire discussions at the **2024** and **2025** Berkshire annual meetings; I cannot tell which (low confidence), and the fragments may come from more than one meeting. Some interjections ("Yeah", "I'd even go, for example") may be from Greg Abel or another person on stage; this is a guess.
