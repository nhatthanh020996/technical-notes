# The Mistake of Selling McDonald's, Why Its Real Estate Can't Easily Be Split Off, and Fast-Food vs. Razor-Blade Loyalty

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4xIxc_Lyn9k

Recorded: most likely 1998 Berkshire annual meeting (moderate confidence)

> Note: The original YouTube title is "Warren Buffett: Why I Regret Selling McDonald's Stock". It is only partly accurate: the first two minutes are about the sale, but Buffett gives no real reasons beyond saying he didn't think McDonald's belonged among the eight or ten businesses Berkshire wants to own, that it was a mistake costing "in the area of a billion dollars plus", and then uses it to talk about postmortems on decisions. Most of the clip is about something else: whether McDonald's should separate its real estate from its franchising business, and why fast food can't have the customer loyalty of Gillette razor blades.
