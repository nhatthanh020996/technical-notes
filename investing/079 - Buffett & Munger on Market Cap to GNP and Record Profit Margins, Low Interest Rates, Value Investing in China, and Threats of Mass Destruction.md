# Buffett & Munger on Market Cap to GNP and Record Profit Margins, Low Interest Rates, Value Investing in China, and Threats of Mass Destruction

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4jHMPQYb2Zo

Recorded: 2015 Berkshire annual meeting (high confidence)

> Note: The original YouTube title is "Warren Buffett: How To Know If Stocks Are Overvalued". It is misleading. Buffett gives no method for knowing whether stocks are overvalued. Asked about two of his old indicators (total market cap to GNP at about 125%, and corporate profits at about 10.5% of GNP), he says the profit figure may be a concern for other parts of society, and that stock prices have to be judged against "incredibly low interest rates": if rates go back to normal, stocks "will look pretty high", and if rates stay this low they "will look very cheap" ("take your pick"). The second half of the clip is about value investing in China and about chemical, nuclear, biological and cyber threats to the United States. The clip is from the **2015** Berkshire annual meeting (high confidence). The clues: Buffett refers to what Charlie said "in that movie" (the 50th-anniversary film shown at the 2015 meeting); the Chinese questioner says the Chinese market "just doubled over the last six months" (late 2014 to spring 2015); government bond yields of about 1% are discussed; GDP per capita is given as $54,000. The question on threats is read by a panelist whom Buffett calls "Carol" (taken here as Carol Loomis, who apologizes to Becky after the CNBC joke).
