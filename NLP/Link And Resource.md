# NLP Learning Resources & Links

Here are valuable resources to support your NLP learning journey:

## Courses

- **Stanford CS224N**: [Natural Language Processing with Deep Learning](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs224n/) - Free lectures, slides, and assignments
- **Fast.ai NLP**: [A Code-First Introduction to NLP](https://www.fast.ai/posts/2019-07-08-fastai-nlp.html)
- **Hugging Face Course**: [NLP with Transformers](https://huggingface.co/learn/nlp-course/chapter1/1)
- **DeepLearning.AI**: [Natural Language Processing Specialization](https://www.deeplearning.ai/courses/natural-language-processing-specialization/)

## Books

- [Speech and Language Processing](https://web.stanford.edu/~jurafsky/slp3/) - Free online book by Jurafsky & Martin
- [Natural Language Processing with PyTorch](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/natural-language-processing/9781491978221/)
- [Practical Natural Language Processing](https://www.practicalnlpbook.com/)

## Online Tutorials & Documentation

- [spaCy Documentation](https://spacy.io/usage/spacy-101)
- [NLTK Book](https://www.nltk.org/book/)
- [PyTorch NLP Tutorials](https://pytorch.org/tutorials/beginner/nlp/deep_learning_tutorial.html)
- [Transformers Documentation](https://huggingface.co/docs/transformers/index)

## GitHub Repositories

- [NLP-progress](https://github.com/sebastianruder/NLP-progress) - Tracking progress in NLP
- [100 Days of NLP](https://github.com/graykode/nlp-tutorial) - NLP tutorials organized by model type
- [Awesome-NLP](https://github.com/keon/awesome-nlp) - Curated list of NLP resources

## Research Paper Collections

- [ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/) - Publications from computational linguistics conferences
- [Papers With Code](https://paperswithcode.com/area/natural-language-processing) - Papers with implemented code

## Blogs & Articles

- [Sebastian Ruder's Blog](https://ruder.io/) - Excellent articles on NLP techniques
- [Jay Alammar's Visualizing ML](https://jalammar.github.io/) - Visual explanations of NLP concepts
- [Towards Data Science - NLP section](https://towardsdatascience.com/tagged/nlp)

## Communities

- [r/LanguageTechnology](https://www.reddit.com/r/LanguageTechnology/) - Reddit community for NLP
- [NLP News](https://newsletter.ruder.io/) - Newsletter about NLP research

These resources cover different learning styles and experience levels, so you can choose what works best for your background and learning preferences.
